# Lanceman's BattleTech Campaign Tools

These are a collection of BattleTech campaign management web apps covering Chaos Campaign (Campaign Operations), Chaos Campaign Mercenaries, and BattleTech Aces. All are HTML and JavaScript based, run in your browser, work offline, and are installable as Progressive Web Apps.  

Code for these tools have been authored with the assistance of agentic AI. No generative AI art assets have been used.

Access at: https://x-lanceman-x.github.io/BTechCampaignTools/

## Features

### Aces Companion
* Digitial version of Aces campaign record sheets with automated calculations to make in-between sortie time easier.
* Deployment tracker show you how much PV you've committed to a Sortie
* Pilot Cards that make managing SP easy

### Chaos Campaign Mercenaires Companion
* Track your mercenary unit as it grows from Scale to Scale
* Track SP spending easily with automated SP expenditure
* Fully supports classic and Alpha Strike
* Supports optional rules like No BSP and Jump Tracking

### Chaos Campaign Companion
* Easily calculate your unit's Equipment Rating
* Track both Warchest and Support Points as you spend them
* Quick Warchest to Support Point Conversion

### Across the board
* Journal feature for notes or your unit's story
* Track multiple campaigns and switch between them whenever you want
* Manual data export and import
* Import MekBay CSV files to quickly populate your forces
* Multiple device syncing using GitHub Gists

## Lanceman's Chaos Campaign Mercenaries Digital Companion Major Update

The CC Mercs Digital Companion has been updated to support new additions from Hot Spots Draconis Reach and add in missing items from Hot Spots Hinterlands.

Major Features:

* Named Pilot cards that track your pilots' progress
* Redone roster for Nameless Pilots
* Support for Contract Scale and Track Scale limitations
* Support for Regular Military Commands
* Hire a DropShip and upgrade its bays
* Hiring Hall bonuses
* The Deployment Area now allows you to "Complete" a deployment, recording the results to the ledger and disbursing combat bonuses and Named Pilot pay
* New Support Costs tab calculator to support Battlefield Loss Compensation rules changes 
 
 Quality of Life Improvements:
 
 * Easily do monthly expenses and travel costs with two new buttons on the Monthly Ledger that takes into account your contract terms and if your unit is at a Hiring Hall
 * A new Verbose Mode option increases the detail recorded in the ledger when Support Cost purchases are made
 
 Note: if loading a force from the Hinterlands only version, any pilots will be converted to Temporary Named Pilots
 
A final Hinterlands only version has been archived at: https://github.com/x-Lanceman-x/BTechCampaignTools/blob/main/docs/Legacy%20Versions/CCMercsContractSheet(Final%20Hinterlands%20Only).html
